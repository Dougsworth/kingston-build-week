Why we gather

The region has more builders than it has rooms for them. Kingston Build Week is that room. Two days by the harbour where engineers, designers and founders from across the Caribbean compare notes, argue about architecture and leave with people they will ship with.

No sales pitches from the stage. Every talk is about something the speaker built, broke or rebuilt here, with the numbers to prove it.

Workshops are small and hands on. Bring a laptop, leave with working code, a sharper product plan or a cap table you finally understand.

Build Week in numbers

1,200 builders, 48 speakers from eleven countries and 24 workshops, across two days by the harbour.

Last year we sold out in nine weeks. This year the hall is bigger and the student allocation has doubled.

Last year at Build Week

Last year in the Main Hall. Every keynote ran standing room only, so this year the hall seats four hundred more.

        Questions

        Who is Build Week for?

        Anyone who makes things with software in the Caribbean. Engineers, designers, product people, founders and the people thinking about becoming one. If you have shipped something, or want to, you belong here.

        Can I get a refund if my plans change?

        Yes, in full until 1 February 2027. After that you can transfer your ticket to a colleague at no cost by emailing us their name.

        Do workshops need a separate booking?

        No. Every ticket includes workshops. Seats are limited to 30 per room, so we open workshop sign up to registered guests two weeks before the event.

        Is there support for students and people travelling from other islands?

        We fund forty travel grants for builders from outside Jamaica and keep student tickets at cost. Applications for grants open in December.

        Is the venue accessible?

        Every room is on the ground floor with step free entry. The Main Hall has live captions on screen, and there is a quiet room beside the Gallery all day.

        Can my company sponsor?

        We keep the partner list short so the hall stays about the work. Write to us and we will send the plan for 2027.
